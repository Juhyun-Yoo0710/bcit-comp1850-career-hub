Learning Resources

This page introduces free learning resources that help individuals develop IT skills and prepare for careers in the technology industry. These programs offer training, certifications, and career support, especially for newcomers looking to enter the Canadian job market.

NPower Canada ↗

NPower Canada is a non-profit organization that provides free technology training programs for job seekers. It offers hands-on learning, industry certifications, and career support to help participants start careers in IT. The program is especially beneficial for newcomers and individuals seeking entry-level tech roles.

ICTC ↗

ICTC (Information and Communications Technology Council) provides training programs and career development resources in the technology sector. It offers courses, certifications, and labour market insights to help individuals build in-demand digital skills. The organization supports job readiness and career advancement in Canada's tech industry.

IECBC-FAST ↗

IECBC-FAST is a government-funded program that helps internationally trained professionals transition into the Canadian tech workforce. It provides fast-track training, certification support, and job search assistance. The program is designed to help participants quickly gain the skills needed for in-demand IT roles.
