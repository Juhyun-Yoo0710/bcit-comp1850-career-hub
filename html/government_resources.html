Free Government Funding Resources

These organizations are funded by the government and provide free services to help newcomers successfully integrate into the Canadian job market.

WorkBC ↗

WorkBC is a government-funded employment service that provides free support to job seekers across British Columbia. It offers career guidance, job search tools, and access to training programs. WorkBC helps newcomers understand the local job market and connect with employment opportunities.

ISSofBC ↗

ISSofBC offers free employment and settlement services funded by the government to support newcomers in British Columbia. Their programs include job search assistance, resume workshops, and career coaching. They also help clients gain Canadian work experience and improve employability.

S.U.C.C.E.S.S. ↗

S.U.C.C.E.S.S. is a government-funded organization that provides a wide range of services for newcomers, including employment support and settlement programs. It offers career planning, job readiness training, and networking opportunities. The organization helps immigrants successfully integrate into the Canadian workforce.

Options Community Services ↗

Options Community Services provides government-funded employment programs designed to support newcomers in their job search. Services include career counselling, resume and interview preparation, and access to work experience opportunities. The program helps clients build confidence and transition into the Canadian workplace.

DIVERSEcity Community Resources Society ↗

DIVERSEcity Community Resources Society offers free employment and settlement services funded by government programs. It provides job readiness training, mentorship, and career support for newcomers. The organization focuses on helping individuals develop skills and find meaningful employment in British Columbia.
