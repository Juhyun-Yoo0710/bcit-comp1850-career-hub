Welcome

I am a software engineer with over 10 years of experience in C#, ASP.NET, enterprise systems. I have focused on improving system reliability, optimizing backend performance, and supporting large-scale, mission-critical applications.

After an 8-year career break, I am now working towards returning to my previous career in Canada. While it has been challenging due to the lack of Canadian experience and rapid changes in the IT industry, I believe that persistence and continuous effort will eventually lead me to my goal.

Through the support of various organizations, I have been gaining valuable experience and learning along the way. I created this website to share my journey and resources, hoping it can help other newcomers and individuals preparing to re-enter the workforce after a career break.

My Journey

  1. LINC (Language Instruction for Newcomers to Canada)

    LINC provides free English language training for newcomers through organizations such as ISSofBC, S.U.C.C.E.S.S., and Options Community Services. As of April 2026, only Levels 1-4 are available, while Levels 5-8 have been discontinued.

    My experience: Completed LINC Levels 6 and 7.

  2. Career Paths for Skilled Immigrants

    This program supports skilled immigrants in finding employment in Canada through career guidance, training, and financial assistance. Support can be provided for up to two years, depending on the program and occupation.

    My experience:

    • Currently participating in the program through ISSofBC
    • Completed Career Paths online courses
    • Completed OSLT (Occupation-Specific Language Training)
    • Received financial support (approximately $2,000 CAD) for training
    • enrolled in BCIT — Web Design & Development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript)
