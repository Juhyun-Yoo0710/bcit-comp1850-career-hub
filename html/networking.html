Networking

Networking is an essential part of the job search process in Canada, especially in the tech industry. Many job opportunities are filled through referrals and connections rather than public job postings. Building professional relationships can help newcomers gain insights, access hidden opportunities, and successfully enter the Canadian job market.

Don't be afraid to start small — many connections begin with a simple conversation.

BC Tech Association ↗

BC Tech Association is a great place to connect with people in the local tech industry. It regularly shares events, programs, and opportunities to meet professionals and companies in British Columbia. For newcomers, it’s a helpful way to start building connections and understand the local tech community.

Meetup ↗

Meetup makes it easy to find local events and groups based on your interests, including many tech-related communities. You can join casual meetups, workshops, or networking events and start conversations naturally. It’s one of the easiest ways to meet people and build connections in a new city.

Web Summit - Vancouver↗

Web Summit Vancouver is a major tech conference where you can meet professionals, startups, and companies from around the world. The 2026 event will take place in May, making it a great opportunity to connect with others and explore the latest trends in the tech industry. Events like this are especially valuable for building real connections and expanding your professional network.

LinkedIn ↗

LinkedIn is one of the most important tools for networking in Canada. You can connect with professionals, join industry groups, and reach out to people working in roles you’re interested in. Staying active and engaging with others can help you build relationships and discover opportunities that aren’t publicly posted.
